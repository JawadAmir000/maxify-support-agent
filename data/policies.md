# Threadline customer policies

## Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The day of delivery is day 0, so day 30 is the
last day. Items that have not been delivered yet cannot be returned.

## Refunds
A full-price item returned within 30 days is refunded for the price paid for that item, to the
original payment method. Refunds above 150.00 are reviewed by a member of staff before any money
moves. An item can be refunded once.

## Sale items
Items bought on sale are exchange-only. The exception is a defective sale item, which can be
refunded within 30 days of delivery. Defect refunds above 60.00, and any second defect claim from
the same customer, are reviewed by a member of staff.

## Exchanges
An item can be exchanged for another size of the same product within 30 days of delivery, if that
size is in stock. An item can be exchanged once, and an exchanged item cannot then be refunded.

## Cancellations
An order can be cancelled while it is still processing; the payment is returned in full. Once an
order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.

## Escalation
When a request needs a person (a review, a fault in our systems, or something these policies do
not cover) it is passed to the support team, who reply by email within 2 business days.
